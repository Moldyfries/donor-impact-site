## Rules that are not obvious from the account names

1. **Restricted vs unrestricted is decided by the donor or the award letter, never by us.** If the
   documentation does not say, it is unrestricted and the item is flagged for the controller.
2. **Snow removal never lands in 5030.** It is a separate vendor and has no budget line, so it is always
   disclosed separately rather than absorbed.
3. **A deposit that mixes ticket revenue and donations must be split**, not posted whole to either.
4. **Credit card processor fees are 5400**, not netted against the donation that generated them.
5. Anything with no supporting document is **uncategorized**, whatever it looks like.
